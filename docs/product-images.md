# Product images: subject, lighting and composition

## Brief structure

Name one product, its materials and orientation. Specify a surface, background, light direction and empty space. An invented product is useful for a concept; an actual catalog item needs a real reference and a fidelity review.

```text
Create a studio campaign image of one unbranded banana-yellow running shoe above a charcoal pedestal. Show the entire shoe, with sharp woven fabric and rubber texture. Light from the upper left, with believable shadow and clear separation from the dark background. Leave empty space for a headline. Do not add a logo or text.
```

## In the generator

Use Text to image for an invented concept. For a real SKU, use reference editing and explicitly preserve silhouette, proportions, label, color, seams and material. Start with one image. Frame the subject so a future crop will not cut it off.

## Review checklist

- Does the product keep its actual number of parts and correct geometry?
- Are texture, material, color and label faithful to the reference?
- Are lighting and contact shadows plausible?
- Can the intended crop keep the product and label visible?
- Does the image invent a feature, accessory or packaging claim?
