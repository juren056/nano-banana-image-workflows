# Reference editing: change the scene, preserve the object

Write two explicit lists: **keep** and **change**. A vague instruction such as "make it premium" leaves too much open.

```text
Use the uploaded ivory serum bottle as the sole product reference. Keep the bottle shape, ivory color, black pump, label wording, label placement and camera angle unchanged. Change only the surroundings: place it on dark stone beside folded golden satin, with a gentle branch shadow and warm side lighting. Keep the whole bottle inside the frame. Add no new logo, words or accessories.
```

## Choose the correct source

Upload [the source-only bottle image](../assets/examples/serum-source.webp). Do not upload the paired before/after illustration as the source: that supplies two competing objects and includes the desired result.

## Review at full size

Compare bottle edges, pump shape, proportions and label letters against the original. Look for haloing, duplicated parts, accidental reflections or changed text. A composition can be attractive while the product is inaccurate.

The paired illustration demonstrates the intended direction, not a recorded editing run. Exact preservation is not guaranteed. See [example and provenance](../examples/02-reference-edit/README.md).

中文要点：分别写出“保留”和“修改”的要求。参考图用单独原图，不能拿对比图当原图；生成后逐一比对瓶身、标签和边缘。
